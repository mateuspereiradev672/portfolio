Motion Design

25 de Abril — Carnation Animation Process

Animation of a Carnation developed at TUMO Coimbra to commemorate the 25th of April Revolution in Portugal. Designed to be displayed in a carousel exhibit, this series illustrates the frame-by-frame evolution of the flower’s blooming sequence.

Stage 1: Line Art & Keyframes

Initial rough animation and timing breakdown of the carnation.

Stage 2: Color & In-Betweening

Adding color fill and smoothing out the frame transitions.

Stage 3: Final Render

Complete loop with detail, shown in the carousel exhibit.
